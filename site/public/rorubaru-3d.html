RORUBARU, the hand roll bar in Wan Chai

RORUBARU is Hong Kong's original hand roll bar, at G/F, 100-102 Queen's Road East, Wan Chai. Tokyo-style hand rolls (temaki) are made to order at the counter with warm seasoned rice and crisp Japanese nori, alongside draught sake and highballs. Bar counter only, open daily 12:00 to 22:00: walk in, or book a seat on Tock.
