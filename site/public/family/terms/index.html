High Rollers — Terms & Conditions

Version 1 (draft)

  1. The programme. High Rollers is RORUBARU's free loyalty programme, run by We Rolling Limited. Membership is personal, one per mobile number, for ages [16+ — to confirm].
  2. Points. You earn points on approved receipts from RORUBARU shops, at the rate shown in the app. The rate depends on your status. Points have no cash value, cannot be transferred, and cannot be exchanged for cash.
  3. Receipts. Each receipt counts once, and only within the time window shown. There is a daily limit. Some receipts are checked by staff before points are added. Receipts that are altered, duplicated or not genuine are rejected.
  4. Visit stamps. You get one stamp per visit, with at most one a day. A full plate (currently 5 stamps) unlocks a visit reward, currently one free hand roll from the regular menu (any except Wagyu), chosen with our staff.
  5. Status. Status is based on what you spent in the last 12 months, at the thresholds shown in the app. Kama is by invitation only.
  6. Rewards. Rewards, extras and their values are shown in the app and may change. A reward can be redeemed once, in person, at a RORUBARU shop. Rewards marked "TBC" are placeholders.
  7. Extras. Gold receipts are random. Friend bonuses are paid once per new member, after their first approved receipt, up to a yearly cap. The birthday roll is available once a year in your birthday week, after your birthday has been on file for the time shown.
  8. Fair use. We may cancel points, rewards or membership that we reasonably believe were obtained by misuse.
  9. Changes and ending. We may change or end the programme with reasonable notice in the app. Rewards you have already unlocked will be honoured for a reasonable period.
  10. Your data. See the Privacy Policy. You can delete your data at any time. Doing so ends your membership and removes your points.
  11. Law. These terms are governed by the laws of the Hong Kong SAR.
