Privacy Policy & Personal Information Collection Statement

Version 1 (draft) · High Rollers loyalty programme

Who we are (the data user)

We Rolling Limited, trading as RORUBARU, a hand-roll bar in Wan Chai, Hong Kong ("we", "us"). We run the High Rollers loyalty programme. Contact for anything about your data: hello@rorubaru.com.

What we collect

  • Mobile phone number (required): your member ID and how you sign in with a one-time code.
  • Name (optional) and language.
  • Birthday (optional, month and day only): for the birthday roll.
  • Receipts: receipt number, total, date and time, shop, and the photo text read on your phone to fill those in. The photo itself is read on your phone and is not uploaded: we keep only what was read from it (date and time, table number, server name, seat count, total, receipt number if one is printed, and the order items with how many). Date, time, table and total together are the receipt's fingerprint, so the same bill can't earn points twice plus a one-way fingerprint of the photo, so the same photo can't be used twice. The fingerprint can't be turned back into the photo. If a future version ever uploads the photo (for example for a dispute), it will be kept only until the receipt is approved, and for no more than 72 hours. [For legal review]
  • Visits, points, status, rewards and redemptions, including which shop and which staff member recorded them.
  • Friend referrals: who invited you, or whom you invited.
  • Your consent choices: what you agreed to, the wording shown and when.

Why we collect it (purposes)

  • To run High Rollers: recognise you, add points and visit stamps, work out your status, and give and redeem rewards.
  • To prevent misuse: for example, each receipt counts once and visits are limited to one a day.
  • To understand how the programme is used, with totals per day and per shop.
  • To serve you at the counter: staff see your number, birthday and spending history so they can recognise you, mark your birthday and suggest what you may like. [For legal review]
  • To personalise suggestions: the order items on receipts you scan (item name and how many, matched to our menu) are used to suggest rewards and dishes you may like on your High Rollers page. The owner can switch this off; items are deleted with your account. [For legal review]
  • Direct marketing only if you opt in (see below).

Giving your phone number is required to join. Everything else is optional.

Direct marketing

We will only send you rewards and member drops on WhatsApp, or push notifications, if you tick the matching optional box. Each one is separate, and none is ticked for you. The marketing would be about RORUBARU food, drinks, events and member offers. We would use your phone number and name for it. You can stop any time, free of charge: untick the box in Your privacy choices on the High Rollers page, tap Unsubscribe from all marketing, reply STOP to any message, or email us. We do not provide your data to anyone else for their own marketing.

Who we share it with

Your data is not sold. RORUBARU staff can see members' phone number, name, birthday and spending history (for example total spend, visits and favourite items), plus points and rewards, to serve you and run the programme. Every time staff look up or open a member's details it is logged (who, which member, when), and the owner reviews that log. [For legal review] It is stored with our database host. If real SMS sign-in codes are switched on, your number is passed to the SMS provider only to deliver the code. We may disclose data where the law requires it.

Where it is stored and for how long

We store it in RORUBARU's own database, hosted in Sydney, Australia. We keep it while you are a member. When you tap Delete my data, your member record and your receipts, visits, rewards and consent records are deleted [draft: within 30 days, including backups]. Records we must keep by law are kept only for as long as the law requires [to confirm in legal review].

If you delete your account (Me → Delete my account), we keep only a one-way coded version of your phone number and the date you deleted, for up to [30 days, or longer to stop repeat bring-a-friend rewards — to confirm], to prevent misuse of rewards. It cannot be turned back into your number, and nothing else about you is kept with it. During that time the number can't rejoin, and when it does rejoin it starts fresh. [For legal review]

Your rights

Under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486), you can ask for a copy of your personal data and ask us to correct it. Email hello@rorubaru.com with the subject "Data access request" or "Data correction request". We may charge a fee for a data access request, but it will not be excessive. You can delete your High Rollers data yourself at any time on the High Rollers page.

Security

Sign-in uses one-time codes. Staff PINs and codes are stored hashed. Database access goes only through controlled functions. No system is perfect: if something goes wrong that affects your data, we will tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will show the new version and ask you to agree again.
