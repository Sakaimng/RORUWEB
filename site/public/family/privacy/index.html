Privacy Policy & Personal Information Collection Statement

Version 1 (draft) · Roru Family loyalty programme

Who we are (the data user)

We Rolling Limited, trading as RORUBARU, a hand-roll bar in Wan Chai, Hong Kong ("we", "us"). We run the Roru Family loyalty programme. Contact for anything about your data: hello@rorubaru.com.

What we collect

  • Mobile phone number (required): your member ID and how you sign in with a one-time code.
  • Name (optional) and language.
  • Birthday (optional, month and day only): for the birthday roll.
  • Receipts: receipt number, total, date and time, shop, and the photo text read on your phone to fill those in. The photo itself is read on your phone and is not uploaded.
  • Visits, points, status, rewards and redemptions, including which shop and which staff member recorded them.
  • Friend referrals: who invited you, or whom you invited.
  • Your consent choices: what you agreed to, the wording shown and when.

Why we collect it (purposes)

  • To run Roru Family: recognise you, add points and visit stamps, work out your status, and give and redeem rewards.
  • To prevent misuse: for example, each receipt counts once and visits are limited to one a day.
  • To understand how the programme is used, with totals per day and per shop.
  • Direct marketing only if you opt in (see below).

Giving your phone number is required to join. Everything else is optional.

Direct marketing

We will only send you rewards and member drops on WhatsApp, or push notifications, if you tick the matching optional box. Each one is separate, and none is ticked for you. The marketing would be about RORUBARU food, drinks, events and member offers. We would use your phone number and name for it. You can stop any time, free of charge: untick the box in Your privacy choices on the Roru Family page, tap Unsubscribe from all marketing, reply STOP to any message, or email us. We do not provide your data to anyone else for their own marketing.

Who we share it with

Your data is not sold. Only RORUBARU staff see the parts they need, such as your masked number, name and rewards at the counter. It is stored with our database host. If real SMS sign-in codes are switched on, your number is passed to the SMS provider only to deliver the code. We may disclose data where the law requires it.

Where it is stored and for how long

We store it in RORUBARU's own database, hosted in Sydney, Australia. We keep it while you are a member. When you tap Delete my data, your member record and your receipts, visits, rewards and consent records are deleted [draft: within 30 days, including backups]. Records we must keep by law are kept only for as long as the law requires [to confirm in legal review].

If you delete your account, we keep a one-way coded version of your phone number for up to [30 days, or longer to stop repeat bring-a-friend rewards — to confirm] to prevent misuse of rewards. It cannot be turned back into your number, and nothing else about you is kept with it. During that time the number can't rejoin, and when it does rejoin it starts fresh. [For legal review]

Your rights

Under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486), you can ask for a copy of your personal data and ask us to correct it. Email hello@rorubaru.com with the subject "Data access request" or "Data correction request". We may charge a fee for a data access request, but it will not be excessive. You can delete your Roru Family data yourself at any time on the Roru Family page.

Security

Sign-in uses one-time codes. Staff PINs and codes are stored hashed. Database access goes only through controlled functions. No system is perfect: if something goes wrong that affects your data, we will tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will show the new version and ask you to agree again.
